## Brand & Style

This design system expresses solidity, productive heritage, and technological rigor for an agroindustrial soybean processing leader. It unites the grounded strength of industrial manufacturing with the institutional prestige demanded by international traders, institutional investors, agricultural cooperatives, and local producers.

### Aesthetic Movement
The design movement is **Modern Industrial Prestige**—a deliberate fusion of clean Swiss structuralism with agricultural warmth. High-density data modules and technical indicators coexist with expansive white space, structured tabular layouts, and deep, rooted forest tones. It evokes the confidence of concrete processing plants, golden soybean fields, and frictionless commodity logistics.

### Emotional Response & Target Audience
- **Target Audience:** Grain brokers, cooperative leaders, logistics partners, agricultural supply chain executives, institutional buyers of soy meal and crude degummed oil, and regional producers.
- **Tone & Mood:** Robust, transparent, institutional, stable, and highly capable. The interface eliminates superficial trends in favor of structural clarity, high data legibility, and refined agro-corporate authority.

## Colors

The color palette directly draws from the operational architecture of grain crushing facilities and the biological vitality of soybean crops:

- **Primary (`#1B4332` - Deep Canopy Forest):** Anchors primary brand touchpoints, top-level navigation, key actions, and dominant card headers. It signals environmental stewardship, stability, and corporate heritage.
- **Secondary (`#E3A824` - Golden Soy Grain):** Applied as an intentional accent for live financial indicators, commodity tickers, highlights, metrics badges, and hover states. Evokes golden harvested grains and warm industrial precision.
- **Tertiary (`#1F2421` - Industrial Graphite):** Provides structural boundaries, dark section backdrops, high-contrast footer panels, and deep typographic hierarchy.
- **Neutral (`#F8F9FA` - Silo Off-White):** Serves as the expansive canvas base, preventing visual fatigue and allowing technical specifications and market data to breathe clearly.

### Functional & Semantic Roles
- **Market Up (Positive):** `#2D6A4F` (Forest Bright Green) for commodity upticks and trading gains.
- **Market Down (Negative):** `#BC4749` (Muted Industrial Brick) for market drops.
- **Surface Elevation:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated cards, complemented by neutral borders (`#E5E7EB` / `#D1D5DB`) to maintain crisp boundaries without heavy dropshadows.

## Typography

The typographic balance bridges institutional executive presence with industrial and financial precision:

- **Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its balanced geometry, crisp terminals, and authoritative architectural posture. It lends grandeur to industrial headlines without sacrificing readability.
- **Body Text (`Inter`):** The standard for neutral, highly legible content across dense analytical descriptions, technical spec sheets, and corporate disclosures.
- **Labels & Financial Tickers (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied exclusively to commodity quotes (CBOT, Paranaguá, Dólar PTAX), technical laboratory specs (protein levels, moisture percentage, crude fiber), and industrial metric tags. The monospaced alignment guarantees zero optical shifting when dynamic live prices fluctuate.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a modular 12-column responsive grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing into a 4-column structure on mobile devices.

### Breakpoints & Hierarchy
- **Desktop (1280px+):** Max-width 1240px container, 12 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `2rem` outer margin. Allows side-by-side product comparisons, dense lab analysis tables, and live trading widgets.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `1.5rem` outer margin. Tickers wrap into dual-row flex ribbons.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, `1rem` margins. All tabular metrics become horizontally scrollable cards or stacked technical rows.

### Density & Cadence
Corporate and marketing sections use spacious intervals (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`) to establish an open, credible executive atmosphere. Conversely, the operational trading bar and product specification sheets use compact intervals (`space-xs` to `space-md`) to optimize data density and scannability.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids soft consumer-grade blurs or floating neomorphic surfaces. Depth is communicated through **structural borders and disciplined architectural layering**:

1. **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Off-white (`#F8F9FA`) background supporting layout blocks.
2. **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Modules):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid hairline border in `#E5E7EB`. Used for product cards, institutional milestones, and metric displays.
3. **Level 2 (Active & Hover Panels):** Subtle ambient shadow (`0 4px 16px -2px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.08)`) with an accented secondary or primary 1px border highlight.
4. **Header & Persistent Ticker:** Dark tertiary container (`#1F2421`) anchored to the top of the viewport with a bottom accent rule (`2px solid #E3A824`), framing the interface with an authoritative horizon line.

## Shapes

The design system implements a **Soft Industrial Geometry (`roundedness: 1`)**. 

- Standard inputs, buttons, and micro-badges use subtle `0.25rem` (4px) radii.
- Content cards, dashboard panels, and images adopt `0.5rem` (8px) corners.
- Highly rounded or pill shapes are forbidden for primary actions, preserving the crisp, squared precision of silo complexes, factory frames, and freight containers.

## Components

### 1. Live Commodity Ticker Ribbon
- **Container:** Dark Industrial Graphite (`#1F2421`) full-bleed ribbon across the top of the viewport.
- **Data Pairs:** Each market item shows the exchange label in `JetBrains Mono` (`label-tech`), commodity name, current value, and delta indicator.
  - *Soybean CBOT (US$/bushel):* Golden Soy `#E3A824` value.
  - *Paranaguá Port (R$/60kg sc):* Off-white `#F8F9FA` value.
  - *Delta Pill:* Tiny square-cornered badge with `#2D6A4F` (positive) or `#BC4749` (negative) text and subtle 10% opacity background.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary:** Forest Green (`#1B4332`) solid background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.25rem` radius, `0.5rem 1.25rem` padding. On hover: shifts to darker `#0F2D1F` with a subtle golden glow border (`#E3A824`).
- **Secondary (Agricultural Accent):** Golden Soy (`#E3A824`) solid background, Graphite (`#1F2421`) text, medium weight, `0.25rem` radius.
- **Tertiary / Outline:** Transparent background with 1.5px solid `#1B4332` border, matching text color, designed for downloading laboratory reports and technical specs.

### 3. Product Cards (Soy Meal, Crude Degummed Oil, Soy Hulls)
- **Structure:** Surface White (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px `#E5E7EB` border and `0.5rem` radius.
- **Header:** Features high-definition photography of the industrial output (golden oil clarity, granular protein meal, bulk pelleted hulls).
- **Technical Spec Table:** Micro-grid listing analytical metrics (e.g., Minimum Protein 46%, Maximum Moisture 12.5%, Crude Fiber 3.5%) using monospaced values paired with neutral labels.
- **Call-to-Action:** Direct quote request button anchored at the bottom edge.

### 4. Operational Plant KPI Indicators
- **Layout:** 4-column balanced stat grid.
- **Metric Figure:** Large display type (`headline-lg` in `#1B4332`) with trailing units (e.g., `2.500`, `ton/dia`).
- **Accent Detail:** 3px vertical accent bar in `#E3A824` to the left of each metric block.
- **Label:** `body-sm` in `#4B5563` providing operational context (e.g., "Capacidade Diária de Esmagamento", "Armazenamento Estático de Grãos").

### 5. Input Fields & RFQ (Request for Quotation) Forms
- **Input Surface:** Pure white field with 1px border in `#D1D5DB`, `0.25rem` corner radius, `0.75rem 1rem` inner padding.
- **Focus State:** 1.5px border in Primary Forest (`#1B4332`) with zero offset ring.
- **Labels:** Crisp `Inter` 14px bold, aligned directly above input containers.

### 6. Institutional Contact & Grain Origination Widget
- **Layout:** Split module combining regional grain reception hours, terminal delivery coordinates, and an interactive WhatsApp/Phone direct line to the grain desk (*Mesa de Operações de Grãos*).
